## Legal/platform gates BEFORE any paying-customer explicit generation
- For India-facing operation, arrange qualified legal review of [IT Act section 67](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15413/1/20_the_information_technology_act%2C_2000%283%29.pdf) and **67A** governing electronic publishing/transmitting of explicit acts. Age and consent do **not alone** guarantee permitted electronic distribution in every jurisdiction. Also review other countries to which the service or users are exposed.
- Review [Telegram Terms](https://telegram.org/tos); they prohibit illegal pornographic content on publicly visible bots/channels. Private delivery alone is not a substitute for legal review, and bot-specific rules may apply.
- Verify age of both users and depicted persons as required by law (mere 18+ toggle is not age verification); reject underage-looking or age-ambiguous sexual content.
- Require documented, informed, revocable adult consent and rights for any identifiable real-person likeness. Block non-consensual intimate images, coercion, trafficking, sexual violence, minors, bestiality and unlawful uses.
- Build private-only delivery, abuse report and takedown path, sensible retention/erasure, credit ledger and provider cost cap. Do not advertise as 'no limits' because legal and provider prohibitions always remain.
- Confirm payment processors and the chosen host independently permit this specific customer-facing service; obtain explicit written provider authorization if terms are ambiguous.
